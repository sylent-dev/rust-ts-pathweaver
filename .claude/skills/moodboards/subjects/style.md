# The style of an interface

Read by the `moodboards` skill for the look of an interface: its colours, its density, its textures, its mood.

## The questions

Ask them numbered, the main one first, each with the answer drawn from the project.

1. **The mood**: a few words (calm, technical, a forge at night, an old map), or a site, an application or a game whose look the owner likes, even from another field.
2. **Light, dark, or both**, and the screen the owner looks on (its width and its scale, written in `docs/technical-choices.md`).
3. **What the interface shows most** (a tree, tables, a map, cards): the references must show the same kind of content, or the look won't carry over.
4. **The world of its users** (a game, a trade, a hobby), whose interfaces they already know: borrow its mood, never its art.
5. **What to avoid**: a look already seen too often in the domain, the colours of a competitor, an effect too heavy for the device (a blur on a tree of 5,000 nodes).
