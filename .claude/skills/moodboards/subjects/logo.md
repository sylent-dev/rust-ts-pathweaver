# A logo

Read by the `moodboards` skill for a logo, a mark or the icon of the browser tab.

## The questions

Ask them numbered, the main one first, each with the answer drawn from the project.

1. **The families to search.** Propose those drawn from the name of the project, and ask which to add or remove:
   - the first letter alone (a monogram: a P for Pathweaver), and the first letter of each word of the name (a W for weaver);
   - the letters joined (a ligature: PW, WP);
   - each word of the name as an image (path: paths, routes, trails; weaver: weaving, threads, knots);
   - what the application does (a passive tree: nodes, graphs, constellations);
   - the world of its users (a game: sigils, runes, emblems);
   - a neutral family (geometric marks, the logos of tools), as a point of comparison.
2. **The mood**: sober, playful, solemn, technical, hand-drawn? A word or a brand the owner likes, even from another field.
3. **The colours**, if already chosen (the tokens of the mock-up): a mark in one colour on the bands of the interface, or with its own colours.
4. **Where it must read**: the tab of the browser (16 px), the app bar (24 px), a large page (128 px); a mark detailed at 128 px is a smudge at 16 px.
5. **What it must stay apart from**: the namesakes of the project (search its name: `pathweaver logo` found the path planner of WPILib), the logos of the domain (the game it serves), the big brands of its letters (WordPress for « WP »).

## The searches

Short slugs of Dribbble, two or three a board, for example:

| Family | Searches |
|---|---|
| A letter alone | `p-monogram`, `letter-p-logo` |
| Two letters joined | `pw-monogram`, `pw-logo` (and the other order) |
| A word as an image | `path-logo`, `route-logo`, `trail-logo`; `weave-logo`, `thread-logo`, `knot-logo` |
| What it does | `constellation-logo`, `node-logo`, `network-logo-mark` |
| The users' world | `sigil-logo`, `rune-logo`, `emblem-logo` |
| Neutral | `geometric-logo-mark`, `developer-tool-logo`, `line-logo-mark` |

## Then

The proposals are 10 to 12 marks in SVG, drawn from the picks, each shown at 16, 24 and 128 px on every style still open, beside the namesakes to stay apart from (question 5).
