# Game data

Where the names and the stats of the game come from, their terms, and how they follow the versions of the game. Checked on 2026-11-16; read by the steps that load game data (4, 5, 6).

## Sources

| Data | Source | Format | Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passive tree: nodes, positions, links, stats, classes and ascendancies | [grindinggear/poe2-skilltree-export](https://github.com/grindinggear/poe2-skilltree-export), published by Grinding Gear Games; the only game data they export officially (see their [Data Exports](https://www.pathofexile.com/developer/docs/data)) | `data.json` (5 MB, 5,153 nodes) and `assets/`; a Git tag per version of the game (`0.4.0`, `0.5.2`) | No license file: made for tool developers, the data stay those of Grinding Gear Games |
| Gems (names, tags, descriptions), uniques, base items, ascendancies | [RePoE](https://github.com/repoe-fork/repoe), the community export of the files of the game, hosted at <https://repoe-fork.github.io/poe2/> (`skill_gems.json`, `base_items.json`, `uniques.json`, `ascendancies.json`…) | JSON, regenerated after each patch (last update on 2026-11-16) | The code is MIT; the data are those of Grinding Gear Games. « The file formats are not final » |
| The stats of the uniques (base, implicits, stats, by version of the game) | [Path of Building (PoE2)](https://github.com/PathOfBuildingCommunity/PathOfBuilding-PoE2), `src/Data/Uniques/*.lua`: the files of the game don't say which stats a unique has (RePoE's `uniques.json` gives only its name, kind and art), so every source is a list kept by people | One block of plain text per unique (its name, its base, then its stat lines as the game words them, with `{variant:…}` per version), 30 files; the 0.5 uniques came 10 days after the data of 0.5 (2026-06-01, then 2026-06-11) | MIT (its notice goes in the credits of the website); the files say « Item data (c) Grinding Gear Games » |

Measured on the corpus (see [build-files-in-the-wild.md](build-files-in-the-wild.md)): every passive, gem, unique and ascendancy id written by a tool is found in these two sources.

The inventory slots aren't in either source: they are a short fixed list (`Weapon1`, `Offhand1`, `Helm1`, `Ring2`, `Flask1`, `Charm1`, `Trinket1`…, the ids of the `Inventories` table of the game), laid out by Pathweaver itself, and their names are texts of the interface.

Rejected:

- **Path of Building (PoE2)** for the tree and the gems: MIT, complete, but its data are Lua files made for its own calculations. Its texts of the uniques are taken (above, 2026-11-20).
- **poe2db.tw**: a website without an API or a license, « Copyright © 2014-2026 PoE2DB » at the foot of its pages, funded by Patreon (checked on 2026-11-20; the line of 2026-11-16 gave it the license of the wiki): copying its pages (scraping) would take its author's work without leave, and break at each change of its pages. Its author could be asked.
- **The wiki of Path of Exile 2** ([poe2wiki.net](https://www.poe2wiki.net)) for the stats of the uniques: an API (Cargo: `cargoquery`, `items`, `explicit_stat_text`), the base and the stats of each unique typed by its editors, under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. Kept as the way out if Path of Building stops: Pathweaver's discreet donations are no commercial use (the owner, 2026-11-20), and the data taken would keep their license and their credit.
- **Extracting the files of the game ourselves** (PyPoE, poe-dat-viewer): what RePoE already does, kept as the way out if RePoE stops.

## How Pathweaver uses them

- The data are converted at build time by a script of the repository into compact files of the website, one folder per version of the game and per language (`en` only in version 1). The browser loads them from Pathweaver, never from GitHub or RePoE: the website keeps working if a source moves or stops.
- Only what Pathweaver shows is kept: the size counts, the passive tree alone weighs 5 MB raw.
- The version of the game of the data is shown in the warnings panel, so that an unknown id can be read as « made for another version ».
- At each version of the game: run the script on the new version, check the corpus, commit the new folder. Path of Exile 2 1.0 (2026-12-11) changes a lot: whether the passive tree is first built on the data of 0.5 or of 1.0 is decided at step 5, from how the steps before it went.
- **The portraits of the classes and ascendancies** (decided by the owner on 2026-11-16, replacing « no image of the game » of the same day): they are cut from the `assets/background-<class>.webp` of the tree export (one sprite of 3,000 × 3,000 pixels per class: the class and each ascendancy, a round portrait of 1,500 pixels, placed by its `.json`), made small at build time, round, and as the largest square inside the disc for the tabs of the open builds (decided by the owner on 2026-11-17, rather than the faces of the game files shown by poe2db, which no license lets a tool reuse), and bundled with the data of each version of the game, since 1.0 may change them.
- **The icons and frames of the passives** (decided by the owner on 2026-11-18, other tools show them and they give the tree its charm): `assets/skills.webp` and `skills-disabled.webp` of the tree export hold the icon of every passive that can be taken, in colour and greyed (676 icons, by `skills.json`: 34 pixels for a small passive, 49 for a notable, 68 for a keystone, half the scale of the game, so a little soft at the deepest zoom); `frame.webp` holds the frames of the notables, keystones, jewel sockets and ascendancies in their states (unallocated, can allocate, allocated). The sheets are kept whole with their index, rather than cut into 676 files, and bundled with the data of each version of the game: a new version is the script run again. The art behind the groups (`mastery-effect-*.webp`, 640 KB; the 365 masteries have no icon of their own) waits in the backlog. The art referenced by RePoE isn't used; other images (gems, uniques) are decided case by case, when a step needs them.
- **The images of the items** (decided by the owner on 2026-11-20, for the inventory): RePoE gives the path of the art of each base item and each unique in the files of the game (`visual_identity.dds_file` of `base_items.json` and `uniques.json`, `Art/2DItems/…`), and serves that art beside its data as PNG and WebP (<https://repoe-fork.github.io/poe2/Art/2DItems/…webp>, about 108 pixels a cell of the inventory, 5 KB). An item is found by the name a hint gives: its `unique_name`, or the first line of its text, the base its author wrote after it removed; on the corpus, 273 hints of 326 (84 %): the 37 uniques and 236 bases, the rest free texts of their authors (« Any Belt », « Flask upgrade lvl 10… »), which have no image. The mock-up takes the images of the build it shows (`tools/make-data.py`, 16 images, 164 KB); the website bundles them with the data of each version of the game.
- **The icons of the gems** (decided by the owner on 2026-11-20, for the card of a gem on hover, and in the Skills tab as the badge of a skill): RePoE gives the path of the icon of each gem in the files of the game (`icon_dds_file` of `skill_gems.json`: `Art/2DArt/SkillIcons/4k/…` for a skill, a square, `Art/2DArt/SkillIcons/Support/4k/…` for a support, a disc; 637 supports of 642 have one) and serves it beside its data as WebP (<https://repoe-fork.github.io/poe2/Art/2DArt/SkillIcons/…webp>, 88 × 88 pixels, 2 to 3 KB). A skill and a support may share the name of their file: they are named after their whole path under `SkillIcons/`. An icon RePoE doesn't serve leaves its gem without one (1 of the mock-up's 108). The mock-up takes the icons of the builds it shows (`tools/make-data.py`, 107 icons, 432 KB, committed with the mock-up and served by it: the page never loads them from RePoE); the website bundles them with the data of each version of the game.

- **The stats of the uniques** (decided by the owner on 2026-11-20, for the card of a slot of the inventory): read from Path of Building at a fixed commit, the current variant of each unique (the last of its `Variant:` or `Version:` lines), its implicits apart (`Implicits: N`), its own lines (league, source, requirements) left out. A unique Path of Building doesn't have yet (the new ones of a version, for about ten days) shows its name, its base and its kind, without stats, until it has them. The mock-up takes the uniques of the build it shows (`tools/make-data.py`); a unique whose variant the player chooses (« Has Alt Variant », 5 of 435) is read properly by the script of step 4.

## Risks accepted

Accepted by the owner on 2026-11-16, with the specifications of version 1:

- **No written policy of Grinding Gear Games for third-party tools.** Their [terms of use](https://www.pathofexile.com/legal/terms-of-use-and-privacy-policy) license the use of the website and the game, not the reuse of its data; the tree export exists for tool developers, and poe.ninja, Path of Building and Maxroll show the same names and stats. Pathweaver uses texts and positions, the portraits of the classes and ascendancies (accepted by the owner on 2026-11-16, knowing that no policy allows it: guide sites and databases use far more of the art of the game, and Grinding Gear Games lets them) and the images of the items (accepted by the owner on 2026-11-20, for the same reason, served by RePoE, a community project, rather than by Grinding Gear Games) and the icons and frames of the passives (accepted by the owner on 2026-11-18, for the same reason; the way out, if Grinding Gear Games asks, is Pathweaver's own shapes for the passives); it states in its README and its pages that it isn't affiliated with Grinding Gear Games, and removes what they would ask to remove.
- **RePoE is run by volunteers.** A late or broken update delays the data of a new version; the way out is to extract the files ourselves.
- **Path of Building is run by volunteers too** (accepted by the owner on 2026-11-20): the stats of the new uniques of a version wait for it, and a mistake of its editors shows in Pathweaver; the way out is the wiki (Rejected, above).
- **The format of the data may change** at any version, for both sources: the conversion script fails loudly rather than producing incomplete data.
